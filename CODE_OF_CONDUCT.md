# Code of Conduct

niji adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Everyone who takes part in this project, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else it gathers, follows it.

In short: be kind, assume good intent, welcome people of every background and level of experience, and take feedback about your own behaviour gracefully. Harassment, insults and publishing someone's private information are not accepted.

## Reporting

Report behaviour that breaks this code to the maintainer at ash.gkwd@gmail.com. Every report is reviewed, and the reporter's privacy is respected. The maintainer follows the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant, from a private correction up to a permanent ban.
